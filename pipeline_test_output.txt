SECTION:
overview

CONTENT:
Heart failure occurs when the heart muscle doesn't pump blood as well as it should. When this happens, blood often backs up and fluid can build up in the lungs, causing shortness of breath. Some heart conditions slowly leave the heart too weak or stiff to fill and pump blood properly, including narrowed arteries in the heart and high blood pressure.
Proper treatment may improve symptoms and help some people live longer. Lifestyle changes can improve quality of life. But heart failure can be life-threatening. Some people may need a heart transplant or a device to help the heart pump blood. Heart failure may also be called congestive heart failure.
